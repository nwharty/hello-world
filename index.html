Stay & tickets

In the city — Homewood Suites by Hilton, Midtown / Times Square

  • Suites with a full kitchen and a separate living area, sleeps up to 6, free hot breakfast — the apartment feel, and a change from your past Upper West Side stay.
  • Theater District — a short walk to the Gershwin and the Intrepid, quick subway to Yankee Stadium. Summer rates ~$350–500/night. Book at hilton.com → Splurge alt: AKA Times Square.

At LEGOLAND — LEGOLAND New York Hotel (Goshen, NY)

  • Themed rooms with a kids' sleeping nook, pool, and nightly shows.
  • Look at the Hotel + 2nd-Day-Free ticket package. Parking ~$35/night. Book at legoland.com/new-york →

🎭 Wicked — Gershwin Theatre

  • Sat Aug 15, 2:00pm, 2 seats (wife + daughter). Mid-tier seats ~$130–180 each.
  • Book early at Broadway.com → No children under 5 admitted (recommended 8+).

⚽ NYCFC soccer — Yankee Stadium, the Bronx

  • Sun Aug 16, 6:30pm vs. Philadelphia Union. In the city — reachable by the 4 or D train, no New Jersey.
  • Tickets on Ticketmaster → (family seats often ~$40–60 each). Sit lower-level near the action for the kids.
