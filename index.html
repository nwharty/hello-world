Where you'll stay

In the city — Homewood Suites by Hilton, Midtown / Times Square

  • Why it's the pick: suites with a full kitchen and a separate living area that sleep up to 6 — the apartment feel your wife wanted, and a real change from last year's Upper West Side stay. Free hot breakfast is a money-saver with three kids.
  • Location: Theater District — a short walk to the Gershwin, Intrepid, and Rockefeller Center. Summer rates run roughly $350–500/night. Book direct at hilton.com →
  • Want to splurge? AKA Times Square is a true apart-hotel (full kitchen + washer/dryer, 1–2 bedroom apartments), roughly $500–800/night — stayaka.com →

A note on Airbnb: NYC's Local Law 18 effectively ended short-stay whole-apartment rentals — a legal Airbnb under 30 nights now requires the host to live in the unit with you. An apart-hotel like the two above is the legal way to get the same kitchen-and-living-room setup.

At LEGOLAND — LEGOLAND New York Hotel (Goshen, NY)

  • Themed rooms (Pirate, Kingdom, LEGO Friends, NINJAGO) with a separate kids' sleeping nook, a pool, and nightly entertainment.
  • Look at the Hotel + 2nd-Day-Free ticket package. Parking ~$35/night. Book at legoland.com/new-york →

Wicked — what to book

🎭
  • Saturday, July 25, 2:00pm, 2 seats (wife + birthday girl). The actual birthday Sunday is dark, and a matinee is far easier with kids — it also frees your afternoon with the younger two.
  • Mid-tier seats run about $130–180 each (~$300–360 total); book early, matinees sell out. Buy from Broadway.com or Telecharge.
  • Age: no children under 5 admitted; recommended 8+. Your birthday girl is right in the sweet spot.
