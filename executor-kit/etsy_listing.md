## Description

**Settling an estate is the hardest job nobody trains you for. This kit is the system that gets you through it — calmly, completely, and in the order that actually matters.**

Created with a finance background and designed for real executors, the Executor & Estate Settlement Kit gives every task, document, dollar, and deadline a place to live, so nothing depends on your memory during the worst month to rely on it.

**WHAT'S INCLUDED (instant download)**

📊 **The 12-tab Estate Workbook** (Excel — uploads perfectly to Google Sheets, instructions included):
- First 30 Days priority checklist — what's urgent, what can wait, and why
- Task & Communication Log — every call and letter, documented
- Key Contacts & Document Locator
- Notifications tracker — incl. certified death certificate tracking (the #1 thing executors run out of)

- Beneficiary Distributions with signed-release tracking
- ⭐ **Automatic Final Accounting summary** — beginning inventory + receipts − disbursements − distributions, calculated for you in the format courts and beneficiaries expect

📘 **The Executor's First 30 Days** (PDF guide): a calm, step-by-step walkthrough of the critical first month — including the three rules that protect you from personal liability and the five mistakes that cost executors their own money.

**WHY THIS ONE**
Most executor templates are a checklist and some empty tables. This kit is built around the part that actually trips people up: **the accounting.** Courts and beneficiaries are entitled to a final accounting of every dollar — and this workbook builds it automatically as you go, instead of leaving you to reconstruct a year of transactions at the end.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Purchase → instant download (no physical item ships)
2. Use in Excel, or upload to Google Sheets (free) — both work fully
3. Start with the guide, then the First 30 Days tab. The workbook does the organizing.

**A NOTE ON WHAT THIS IS**
This is an organizational tool, not legal or tax advice — rules vary by state. It's designed to keep you so organized that when you do need an attorney or CPA, they bill fewer hours.
