# Niche Research — Executor / Estate Settlement Digital Products (June 2026)

## Why this niche

- Demand confirmed: bestseller listings with strong, emotional reviews ("I can't even tell you how helpful this has been… even though I have a lawyer helping"). Buyers are motivated, in a stressful life event, and price-insensitive in the $15–30 range.
- Competition is a handful of credible sellers plus template mills — not the thousands of sellers in budgeting/planner niches.
- Profile fit: pure digital download, near-zero customer contact, build-once.

## What top sellers include (baseline to match)

The leading "After Death Spreadsheet" (made by a 20-yr probate lawyer) includes: instructions, progress checklist, auto financial summary, assets (multi-category), debts, services/subscriptions, expenses, distributions, task tracker, document whereabouts. Excel and Google Sheets sold as separate listings.

## Gaps found (our differentiators)

1. **Final accounting** — courts/beneficiaries are entitled to a formal accounting (beginning inventory + receipts − disbursements − distributions = ending balance). No major listing centers this; ours calculates it automatically. This is the genuinely hard part of the job.
2. **Emotional onboarding** — reviews show the value buyers feel is *relief from overwhelm*. A "First 30 Days" guide that sequences the job addresses this directly; most competitors ship tables with no narrative.
3. **Death certificate tracking** — universally cited pain point (need 10–15 certified copies, every institution wants one); no competitor tracks where copies went.
4. **Both formats in one purchase** — competitors split Excel/Google Sheets into two listings.
5. **Liability framing** — the three rules (no commingling, no early distributions, document everything) are what attorneys say actually burns executors; making them prominent builds trust and review-worthy value.
